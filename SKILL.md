# Photo Marker Memory Poster

## Purpose
Create a reusable 3:4 memory poster from exactly one source photograph.

The poster combines:
- upper half: faithful original photography
- lower half: hand-painted marker reinterpretation of the same instant
- short handwritten English memory caption
- minimal doodles
- current system date in `YYYY.MM.DD`

## Non-negotiable source rule
The uploaded photograph is the **single source of visual truth**.

Never import, reference, combine, replace, or invent people, faces, clothing, poses, gestures, props, environmental objects, events, or scenery from any other image.

Do not add a person or object simply because it would improve composition.

## Default output
- Orientation: portrait
- Aspect ratio: 3:4
- Split: approximately 50% photography / 50% illustration
- Divider: clean, straight, unmistakable horizontal boundary
- No decorative frame unless requested

## Step 1 — Read the photograph
Before generating, visually inventory only what is actually visible:
- number of people
- apparent facial features and expressions
- gaze direction
- hairstyles
- head orientation
- body poses
- hand gestures
- clothing and footwear
- carried/held objects
- relative positions and scale
- foreground/background relationships
- camera perspective
- dominant lighting
- key environmental structures and colors

Do not infer unseen details.

## Step 2 — Upper photographic half
Preserve the source photograph faithfully.

Keep unchanged:
- identity
- facial geometry
- expression
- gaze
- hairstyle
- pose
- gesture
- clothing
- body proportions
- objects
- environment
- perspective
- lighting logic
- composition
- original moment

Allowed:
- extremely subtle global color balancing
- minimal crop/fit adjustment required for the 3:4 composition

Forbidden:
- face beautification
- facial reconstruction
- changing age
- changing body shape
- changing clothing
- removing source objects
- adding objects
- changing gestures
- changing expressions
- repainting the photographic half

## Step 3 — Lower illustrated half
Repaint the **same photographed instant**, not a new scene.

The lower-half people must correspond one-to-one with the people above.

Preserve:
- same identities
- same number of people
- same expressions
- same gaze logic
- same poses
- same gestures
- same hairstyles
- same clothing
- same props and object relationships
- same scene direction
- same relative placement

### Identity lock
The illustrated subjects are hand-painted representations of the exact people in the source photograph, not newly designed characters.

Preserve as closely as the medium permits:
- facial geometry
- face width
- eye spacing
- eye shape
- nose shape
- mouth shape
- hairline
- hairstyle
- apparent age
- expression
- head orientation
- body proportions

Do not beautify, redesign, age up/down, replace faces with generic illustrated faces, or alter perceived ethnicity.

## Painting language
Use visibly handmade media:
- broad marker
- acrylic paint marker
- opaque gouache
- dry brush
- chunky short strokes
- overlapping pigment
- imperfect edges
- intentional exposed paper

Avoid polished digital painting.

### Face palette
Build faces with large, short, tactile strokes using colors such as:
- peach
- coral pink
- cream
- warm orange
- warm brown

No smooth digital skin gradient.

### Hair
Use broad strokes of:
- black
- brown
- navy

Keep the source hairstyle recognizable.

### Clothing
Retain source clothing colors and construction, simplified into broad marker blocks and visible strokes.

## Illustrated background
Use warm ivory art paper as the base.

Show:
- paper grain
- pigment friction
- layered paint
- dry-brush marks
- small areas of exposed paper

Extract only the source photograph's key environmental structures, directions, and dominant colors.

Reorganize them as bold, irregular painted shapes.

Do not create a literal second photographic copy of the background.

Do not invent landmarks, buildings, plants, animals, weather, vehicles, or scenery absent from the source.

## Lower-half composition
- People should occupy roughly 70–85% of the lower illustrated area when practical.
- Preserve recognizable relative scale between subjects.
- Leave intentional ivory negative space around the illustration for the caption and doodles.
- Do not let typography obscure important faces or gestures.

## Caption generation
Generate one original English memory line based only on the visible mood/activity of the photograph.

Desired qualities:
- natural
- everyday
- affectionate where appropriate
- lightly humorous where appropriate
- travel/memory feeling where appropriate
- short enough to hand-letter

Do not repeatedly use a fixed stock phrase.
Do not state specific locations, relationships, names, dates, or events unless visibly established or supplied by the user.

Optional: add one much smaller secondary line.

## Typography
Main caption:
- large handwritten broad-marker lettering
- organic spacing
- visibly handmade

Secondary caption:
- small handwritten text
- visually subordinate

Add 1–3 minimal doodles selected to fit the image, such as:
- heart
- small sun
- smile
- short underline

Keep doodles simple and hand-drawn.

## Date
Place the generation day's system date in the lower-right corner.

Format exactly:
`YYYY.MM.DD`

Style:
- very small handwritten numerals
- unobtrusive

## Quality checks before final generation
Verify:
1. Exactly one source photograph controls all visual content.
2. No extra people have appeared.
3. No source person has disappeared.
4. Upper half remains photographic and faithful.
5. Lower half depicts the same moment rather than a new pose.
6. Faces remain recognizably similar.
7. Clothing and props match the source.
8. Illustration has physical marker/gouache texture, not smooth digital rendering.
9. Background is simplified but source-grounded.
10. Main caption is short and original.
11. Date uses current system date.
12. Final format is portrait 3:4 with a clean horizontal split.
